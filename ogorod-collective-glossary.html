Ogorod: Collective Glossary

Riso-printed zine / 205 × 105 mm / April 2025

Ogorod (Russian for "vegetable garden") is a collective glossary created during the workshop "From the Place of Soils" at the Revolt Centre in Syktyvkar, Komi Republic in July 2024.

The zine gathers cultivation practices and soil-related knowledge passed down by women in post-socialist rural contexts. Through collaborative writing, drawing, and sharing of experiences, workshop participants created a living archive — documenting embodied, everyday, and historical forms of resilience.

The publication is riso-printed on recycled paper, reflecting the project's commitment to sustainable and cooperative knowledge practices.

Design & production: Workshop participants, Revolt Centre
Format: Riso-print, 205 × 105 mm
Edition: 60
