Soil, Chapter One — Video Essay (2025)

"The Soil. Chapter One" looks into the land as memory, method, and material. Moving between personal memory, artistic research interviews and rural every day practices, the experimental video essay reflects on soil as a living archive shaped by resistance. It is Field-based documentation in work - please don't share the link.
