Learning from the cultivation practices of Eastern European women in rural spaces

The research learns from the rural cultivation practices of Eastern European women through artistic fieldwork and a feminist vision. 

It examines the topography of cultivation practices in two regions with a history of socialist collective labour strategies: Komi Republic (Russia) and Armenia. The study is conducted with autobiographical artistic methods, linking the regions through a history of personal migration.
